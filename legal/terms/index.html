Terms and Ethics

Gaymer.Social and Gaymer.Coffee, the Mastodon instances this site now covers, were discontinued in September 2026. This site is not a live service — it's a static, read-only notice explaining that.

No accounts, no service

There is nothing to sign up for, log into, or use here. Because there's no service being offered, there are no acceptable-use terms to agree to beyond the general expectation that you use the web responsibly.

Where the real community rules used to live

While Gaymer.Social and Gaymer.Coffee were operating, each instance had its own Code of Conduct and rules governing its actual social network. Those governed community behaviour on the Mastodon software itself, not this notice site, and no longer apply to anything since both instances are offline.

Changes

If anything material about this notice changes, it will be reflected on this page and on the homepage.
