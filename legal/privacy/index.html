Privacy Policy

The short version: this site collects nothing about you.

What this site is

This page is a static notice explaining that the Gaymer.Social and Gaymer.Coffee Mastodon instances were discontinued in September 2026. It has no accounts, no forms, no database, and no server-side code of its own.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no analytics scripts, no tracking pixels, and no third-party embeds on this site. A small inline script checks whether you're viewing the page from localhost/127.0.0.1 to show a local-development banner — that check runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

Hosting logs

This site is served as static files by a third-party host. That host may keep its own standard web server access logs (IP address, request time, requested path) under its own privacy policy, the same as any other website — we don't have access to or control over those logs beyond what the host provides.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected on this page.
