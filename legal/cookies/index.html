Cookies Policy

This site sets no cookies at all — not for tracking, not for preferences, not for anything.

It doesn't use localStorage, sessionStorage, or any other client-side storage either. The only client-side script on this site is a small inline check of your browser's current hostname and URL, used purely to decide whether to show a "local development build" banner — it reads nothing from and writes nothing to your browser's storage.

No third-party scripts, embeds, or fonts are loaded from anywhere else, so there are no third-party cookies to disclose.
