Service Discontinued

Gaymer.Social is no longer available

Gaymer.Social and Gaymer.Coffee, our Mastodon instances for LGBTQ+ gaymers, were discontinued in September 2026.

Rising running costs, combined with the loss of hosting infrastructure in the NorthC data centre fire, made it impossible to keep either instance online. As a result of the fire, no export or migration of account data was possible before the shutdown.

We're not aware of a successor community and aren't recommending one. Thank you to everyone who was part of Gaymer.Social and Gaymer.Coffee.
